# M62 - Stem crop growth

**Status:** Closed locally; M62.a through M62.b landed. Branch:
`dev/M62-stem-crop-growth`, stacked after local M61 closeout.
**Owning crates:** `mc-net` for age-growth planning and focused tests; `docs`
for readiness and closeout.

## Goal

Extend the existing deterministic age-growth path to melon and pumpkin stems so
survival farms have visible pre-fruit progression beyond the basic crops.

## Scope

- Add random-tick and bonemeal age growth for `minecraft:melon_stem` and
  `minecraft:pumpkin_stem`.
- Advance one `age` value per successful growth, stopping when the registry has
  no next age state.
- Preserve existing crop behavior and bonemeal consumption semantics.
- Do not generate melon or pumpkin fruit blocks yet.

## Non-Goals

- Attached stem conversion or fruit placement.
- Vanilla RNG/growth-rate/light/soil parity.
- Stem harvest/drop parity.
- Particles, sounds, statistics, game events, or level events.
