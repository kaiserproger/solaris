# M76 - Plant lifecycle readiness bucket

**Status:** Completed locally. Branch: `dev/M76-plant-lifecycle-readiness`,
stacked after local M75.
**Owning crates:** primarily `mc-net` and `mc-test-harness`, with readiness docs.

## Goal

Take a broader plant/farming slice than M75 by closing the high-value M72 leftovers
that are still unit-covered only or explicitly unclaimed in replacement readiness.
M76 should improve real survival wire coverage for visible plant lifecycle paths and
tighten small deterministic support rules where the change is cheap and testable.

M76 still preserves Solaris' local deterministic plant semantics. It is not a full
vanilla plant parity milestone: no vanilla RNG/age-counter modeling, no loot-table
execution for crop drops, no particles/sounds/statistics/game events, and no large
feature rewrite.

## Priority Backlog

### M76.a - Pumpkin Stem Lifecycle Harness

Promote the pumpkin-stem fruit lifecycle from unit-only confidence to real survival
wire coverage.

Scope:

- Extend or add a stable harness that bonemeals a mature pumpkin stem.
- Assert the stem converts to the expected attached-stem state and a pumpkin block
  appears through real block update/delta packets.
- Preserve the current deterministic fruit direction policy unless the slice finds
  a small, oracle-backed correction.

Non-goals:

- Vanilla random fruit-position selection.
- Exact support/light/moisture checks for stem survival.

### M76.b - Cocoa Harvest Harness

Cover cocoa break/drop behavior through the real play path instead of unit-only drop
tests.

Scope:

- Materialize mature cocoa on a valid jungle-log side in the harness world.
- Break it through normal survival actions.
- Assert the block clears and deterministic cocoa bean drops are visible and
  pickable through the existing item-entity/pickup path.

Non-goals:

- Vanilla cocoa loot RNG or fortune parity.
- Full cocoa shape/support survival parity after neighboring log edits.

### M76.c - Cactus And Bamboo Visible Growth Harnesses

Expand vertical-plant random-tick coverage beyond sugar cane.

Scope:

- Add stable survival harness coverage for cactus random-tick growth.
- Add stable survival harness coverage for bamboo random-tick growth.
- Reuse the sugar cane harness pattern and keep the local height-three cap.

Non-goals:

- Vanilla age-counter/timing parity.
- Bamboo stage/leaf-size transitions unless already encoded in loaded block states.
- Cactus side-neighbor damage/player collision behavior.

### M76.d - Small Support Semantics Tightening

Address obvious deterministic support mistakes only when they are small and covered.

Scope:

- Audit sugar cane, cactus, and bamboo support helpers against current readiness
  exclusions.
- Prefer minimal rules that align player-visible survival expectations: cactus
  should not grow when horizontally blocked, and sugar cane water adjacency should
  be enforced only if the existing world/block lookup makes it straightforward.
- Add unit tests for any behavior change and keep harness coverage on real visible
  paths.

Non-goals:

- Full vanilla block-survivability graph.
- Broad rewrite of random-tick sampling.

### M76.e - Readiness Closeout

Update documentation to reflect the broader covered plant lifecycle bucket.

Scope:

- Move pumpkin stem lifecycle, cocoa harvest, cactus random-tick growth, and bamboo
  random-tick growth from unit-only gaps to harness-covered partial support if the
  slices land.
- Record any support-semantics changes and remaining deterministic divergences.
- Run full workspace gates before closeout.

## Closeout Notes

M76 deliberately took a wider plant readiness bucket than M75:

- Pumpkin stem bonemeal now has survival wire coverage for attached-stem conversion
  plus pumpkin fruit placement.
- Mature cocoa harvest now has survival wire coverage for block clear, visible
  deterministic cocoa bean drops, and pickup.
- The vertical plant random-tick harness now covers sugar cane, cactus, and bamboo.
- Sugar cane random-tick growth now requires adjacent base water, and cactus growth
  rejects horizontally blocked target cells.

Remaining intentional gaps:

- Plant/crop drops remain deterministic Solaris yields, not vanilla loot/RNG/fortune
  parity.
- Sugar cane still does not enforce the exact vanilla support-block whitelist.
- Existing cactus blocks do not run full side-neighbor survivability after arbitrary
  neighbor edits, and cactus damage/collision behavior is still out of scope.
- Bamboo stage/leaf-size transitions, vanilla age counters, particles, sounds,
  statistics, and game events remain out of scope.
