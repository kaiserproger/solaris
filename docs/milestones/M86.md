**Status:** Planned. Quality label: `draft`.

## Goal

Make scoped combat, equipment, damage, XP, and effects credible for the
core MVP without broad balance claims.

## Scope

- Damage-source model for melee, projectile, fall, fire/lava, drowning,
  suffocation, starvation, and explicit explosion placeholder/non-goal.
- Armor mitigation and durability.
- Weapon, bow, and shield durability; shield axe-disable if scoped.
- Bow/arrow pickup, despawn, attribution, visible metadata, and sticking
  if oracle-backed.
- Basic effects/potions only if scoped.
- Reconcile deferred projectiles, status-effect sync, armor facts, shield
  timing/angle, sounds/particles, and unsupported damage sources.

## Acceptance

- Combat scenarios cover player vs mob, mob vs player, projectile,
  shield, death, drops, XP, and two-client visibility.
- Bow/shield remains partial unless a specific claim has oracle or
  real-client evidence.
- Shield block durability uses `floor(blocked damage) + 1` for projectile
  and hostile-melee blocks, emits player inventory slot updates, and clears
  active shield use when the shield breaks; axe-disable, exact
  timing/angle, sounds/particles, and broader damage-source parity remain
  unclaimed.
- XP curve/orb lifecycle is vanilla-like and tested or documented as
  divergence.
