# M45 - Manual physics feel gate

**Status:** active on `dev/M45-manual-physics-feel`, cut from `main` at tag
`m44`. Owner-run PrismLauncher gate is prepared but not run by the agent.
**Owning crates:** `mc-test-harness` for any new observable checks, `mc-net` for
play-state movement and packet behavior, `mc-physics` only if a vanilla-backed
movement primitive must change.

## Goal

Turn the remaining M40/M41 owner-facing physics symptoms into a repeatable manual
gate and fix only the pieces that can be tied to a vanilla or runtime-state
oracle.

## Scope

- Player swimming feel and pose transitions in shallow/deep water.
- Server/client correction behavior during sprint-swim, surfacing, and leaving
  water.
- Manual PrismLauncher 26.1.2 notes captured as a stable checklist.
- Optional runtime-side assertions for player water state if they make the manual
  symptom testable.

## Non-Goals

- Bit-perfect vanilla movement constants.
- Boats, vehicles, projectiles, bubble columns, or broad aquatic gameplay.
- Collision/fall/fluid parity unrelated to the manual water-feel gate.

## Strategy

1. Start with the M44 water captures and owner manual report.
2. Add a checklist that distinguishes visual feel, wire corrections, and server
   pose/state.
3. Capture any missing vanilla signal before changing movement behavior.
4. Prefer one minimal runtime fix at a time, with a harness assertion or explicit
   manual-gate note.

## M45.a Manual Gate Definition

Run Solaris in debug mode:

```sh
RUST_LOG=mc_server=info,mc_net::server=debug,mc_net::lock_metrics=warn \
  cargo run --bin mc-server -- --config example.toml
```

Connect PrismLauncher 26.1.2 to `127.0.0.1:25565`. Use the same route as the
M44 water probes when the loaded world contains the deterministic fixture cells:

1. Shallow entry: move into `x=-5..-1, y=64, z=-2..1`, start sprinting, hold
   forward+jump.
2. Deep swim: move through `x=-5..-1, y=64..66, z=3..6`, look slightly up and
   down while holding sprint+forward+jump.
3. Surface/exit: swim upward in the deep column, then leave onto adjacent dry
   land around `x=0, y=64, z=4..5`.

If `example.toml` points at a world that does not contain those cells, first run
the same actions in the nearest shallow and deep water body and record the exact
coordinates as `needs capture`; do not change movement behavior from that manual
run alone.

Expected observations:

| Bucket | Observation | Visibility |
|---|---|---|
| Pass | No repeated rubber-band or extra `SynchronizePlayerPosition` while entering, sprint-swimming, surfacing, or leaving water. | wire-visible via `wire-probe` or harness frames |
| Pass | Local sprint-swim stays client-predicted: no self `SetEntityMotion`, no self `ClientboundSetEntityData`, and no extra `SynchronizePlayerPosition` while entering/swimming/exiting. | wire-visible via `wire-probe` or harness frames |
| Pass | PrismLauncher feels usable: player can enter water, sprint-swim, surface, and leave water without bottom-walking being the only movement path. | visual/manual only |
| Fail | Client is repeatedly corrected, snapped back, or stuck in the wrong pose during the route. | wire-visible when paired with captures |
| Fail | Player visually cannot sprint-swim, cannot surface, or remains swimming on land. | visual/manual first, then needs capture/assertion |
| Needs capture | Exact pitch-driven lift, camera feel, local animation smoothness, sounds, and bit-perfect swim speed. | not proven by current wire captures |

The existing M44 captures for `player-shallow-water-entry`,
`player-deep-water-swim`, and `player-water-surface-exit` remain correction
oracles only. They proved that neither vanilla nor Solaris emitted post-input
player `SetEntityMotion` or extra `SynchronizePlayerPosition` in the scripted
windows, not that the local client feel is identical.

## M45.b Player Water State Assertions

`physics_validation::shallow_water_entry_keeps_self_motion_client_predicted` and
`physics_validation::deep_water_swim_and_exit_keep_self_motion_client_predicted`
exercise the real Play path against the deterministic fixture. They move the
local client through shallow entry, deep sprint-swim, upward swim, and exit-to-land
windows, then fail if Solaris sends self `SetEntityMotion`, self
`ClientboundSetEntityData`, or an extra `SynchronizePlayerPosition` correction in
that window.

This matches fresh vanilla 26.1.2 captures, kept local and uncommitted:
`.analysis/physics-oracles/m45-player-deep-water-swim.vanilla.capture` and
`.analysis/physics-oracles/m45-player-water-surface-exit.vanilla.capture`. After
`PlayerCommand(StartSprinting)`, `PlayerInput(forward+jump+sprint)`, the scripted
swim-up move, and the scripted exit-to-land move, vanilla emits no player self
motion, no player self metadata, and no extra player correction. M44
`wire-probe` scripts stay unchanged and continue to be the vanilla/Solaris
correction comparison tool.

## M45.c Minimal Player Feel Fix Decision

No movement constants changed in M45. The minimal capture-backed runtime change
is that Solaris no longer sends server-authored self water motion or self pose
metadata to the local player during water movement; vanilla keeps that local
movement client-predicted. Remote observers still receive player pose/movement
through the normal visibility path.

The remaining owner-facing swimming feel symptoms are visual/manual until the
owner-run PrismLauncher route records a concrete failure. Any future feel fix
must cite that checklist entry plus a vanilla capture or runtime assertion that
exposes the same state boundary.
