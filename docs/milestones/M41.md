# M41 - Frozen world manual regression investigation

**Status:** automated investigation slice implemented locally; owner
PrismLauncher manual confirmation remains pending. The server was previously
stopped after the owner confirmed that the manual PrismLauncher symptoms
remained after the first local M40/M41 candidate fixes.
**Owning crates:** `mc-net` for play tick/session/entity/drop dispatch,
`mc-physics` for entity motion through blocks/fluids, `mc-protocol` for any
missing or incorrect play packet shapes, `mc-test-harness` for wire-level
reproduction, and `docs` for this record.

## Problem

The vanilla 26.1.2 client still makes the world look stopped during manual play:

- Broken blocks do not produce visible falling/collectible drops for the player.
- Animals and mobs stand still instead of wandering or reacting.
- The scene looks static even though the server process is alive and accepts the
  client connection.

This is a manual-client regression that the current automated baseline does not
catch. Passing `cargo test --workspace`, clippy, and focused unit tests is not
enough to claim the issue fixed.

## Recent Attempts That Did Not Solve It

Two local fixes were attempted and validated by tests, but the owner reported the
same manual symptom afterwards:

- The play loop now sends `ClientboundSetTime` on entry and periodically after
  entry, with a wire test proving the client can receive increasing world time.
- Entity physics was bounded to loaded player chunks and a per-tick query budget
  so thousands of restored entities no longer stall the server while no players
  are active.

Useful observations from those attempts:

- Before the entity-budget change, logs showed `restored=2877` entities and
  `entity_queries=2877`, with runtime ticks taking seconds. That was a real
  server-side stall risk.
- After the entity-budget change, logs without players showed `entity_queries=0`
  and `entity_physics_us` near zero, so that specific no-player stall was reduced.
- The manual bug persisted, which means the visible frozen-world symptom has at
  least one additional cause.

## Working Hypotheses

These are hypotheses, not conclusions:

- Loaded-chunk/session state may not mark the player's active chunks early enough,
  so entity physics and visibility dispatch may be skipped even while the player
  sees the terrain.
- Entity movement may be computed but not sent, filtered, or encoded in a shape
  the 26.1.2 client applies.
- Item drops may spawn and get picked up/despawned too quickly, may be sent before
  the client considers the chunk ready, or may lack required metadata after spawn.
- The manual player may be in Creative for part of the test, where block drops are
  intentionally different from Survival, so the reproduction must record mode and
  exact action sequence.
- A protocol decode/layout issue after some clientbound packet could leave the
  client ignoring later entity/drop packets while still rendering already-loaded
  chunks.
- The server may be alive but behind on the specific per-session play loop because
  chunk streaming, frame drains, or outbound command queues starve interactive
  packets.

## Local Automation Slice

- Runtime pressure logs now include cumulative `visibility_command_drops` in
  addition to entity spawn/move/data/take/remove dispatch counters.
- `mc-net` unit coverage proves the drop counter advances when a visibility
  command cannot be queued to a play session.
- The block-drop wire harness now asserts the completed break update/ack arrive
  before item spawn and that item pickup/removal do not arrive before a short
  visible window after item metadata.
- The existing mob motion harness still proves at least one visible server-owned
  passive mob emits real movement over TCP while the chunk is loaded.

## Investigation Strategy

M41 must reproduce the manual symptom before adding more gameplay patches.

1. Capture the manual session.
   Record server logs from process start through the failed block break and failed
   mob movement. Keep raw captures under `.analysis/` only.

2. Log the relevant live counters while a player is connected.
   At minimum capture `sessions`, `ticketed_chunks`, `prepared_chunks`,
   `entity_queries`, `entity_steps`, `server_entities`, `tick_us`, and outbound
   spawn/move/drop packet counts.

3. Build a wire reproduction for visible drops.
   The harness must mine a normal Survival block and assert the real packet path:
   block update/ack, `AddEntity`, item-stack metadata, pickup delay,
   `ClientboundTakeItemEntity`, `RemoveEntities`, and inventory update ordering.

4. Build a wire reproduction for mob motion.
   The harness must connect, wait for visible server-owned mobs, then prove that at
   least one nearby mob emits movement over time while the chunk is loaded.

5. Only patch the layer the reproduction proves broken.
   Do not add more speculative time/AI/physics changes based on screenshots alone.

## Acceptance

- The server is stopped before replacing binaries or rerunning manual gates.
- A failing automated or captured manual reproduction exists for block drops and
  mob motion.
- The reproduction distinguishes missing server simulation from missing clientbound
  packets and from client ignoring malformed packets.
- A fix makes the reproduction pass and then passes the PrismLauncher manual gate:
  break a Survival block, see the dropped item, see it pick up, and see at least
  one nearby mob move.
- Baseline remains green:
  `cargo test --workspace`, `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings`,
  and `cargo fmt --all -- --check`.

## Not Done / Deferred To M43

M41 reduced the frozen-world/TPS investigation risk and added runtime counters,
but the owner manual gate still has unresolved gameplay problems:

- Player water physics still does not work correctly.
- After a local water recovery slice, the owner re-tested and still reports that
  player swimming/water physics do not work. The current `SetEntityMotion` assist
  is documented as insufficient, not finished behavior.
- Block drops are only partially fixed: dropped items appear, but the behavior is
  still poor.
- Sugar cane does not break correctly.
- Mobs move, but movement still needs manual polish. Fish received an aquatic
  wander/velocity slice after the sideways-swimming report, but it still requires
  PrismLauncher confirmation.
- TPS is still high in the manual scenario, though the owner reports it is now more
  tolerable than before.

These are not considered finished M40/M41 behavior. Carry the gameplay fixes and
polish into M43.
