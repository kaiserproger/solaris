# M63 - Sweet berry bush growth

**Status:** Closed locally; M63.a through M63.b landed. Branch:
`dev/M63-sweet-berry-growth`, stacked after local M62.
**Owning crates:** `mc-net` for age-growth planning and focused tests; `docs`
for readiness and closeout.

## Goal

Add visible growth progression for sweet berry bushes through existing crop
random-tick and bonemeal paths.

## Scope

- Add `minecraft:sweet_berry_bush` to supported age-growth crops.
- Preserve one-age-per-growth behavior and bonemeal consumption only after
  successful growth.
- Stop at the highest age state available in the loaded block registry.

## Non-Goals

- Right-click berry harvesting.
- Bush damage/slowness collision behavior.
- Exact vanilla growth RNG/light rules, particles, sounds, statistics, or game
  events.
