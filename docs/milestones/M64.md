# M64 - Cocoa growth

**Status:** Closed locally; M64.a through M64.b landed. Branch:
`dev/M64-cocoa-growth`, stacked after local M63.
**Owning crates:** `mc-net` for age-growth planning and focused tests; `docs`
for readiness and closeout.

## Goal

Allow cocoa pods to advance through their age states while preserving orientation.

## Scope

- Add `minecraft:cocoa` to supported age-growth crops.
- Preserve non-age state properties such as `facing` when advancing age.
- Reuse random-tick and bonemeal growth semantics.

## Non-Goals

- Cocoa placement on jungle logs.
- Cocoa harvest/drop parity.
- Exact vanilla growth RNG/light/support parity, particles, sounds, statistics,
  or game events.
