# M43 - Vanilla physics validation

**Status:** closeout ready on `dev/M43-vanilla-physics-validation` from `main` at
`d0f77a4` (`feat: M42 lock-free ownership rewrite`). This milestone remained a
validation and oracle milestone, with one scoped Solaris parity fix proven by a
vanilla-backed harness.
**Owning crates:** `mc-test-harness` for wire-level and scenario validation,
`mc-physics` for physics probes, `mc-net` for observable play behavior,
`mc-world` for block/fluid test worlds, and `tools` only for local oracle capture
scripts.

## Goal

Build an authoritative validation suite for vanilla 26.1.2 observable physics so
M44 can fix real mismatches instead of adding speculative movement patches.

M43 must answer: what does vanilla do on the wire and in persisted/world-visible
state for the physics cases Solaris currently approximates or fails manually?

## Current Foundation

- ADR 0002 requires packet layouts and protocol metadata to come from local Mojang
  oracles, not memory.
- M40/M41 identified unresolved manual gaps: player water physics, poor block-drop
  feel, sugar cane breaking, fish/aquatic movement polish, mob movement polish,
  and high TPS under manual scenarios.
- Current tests prove selected Solaris paths, but they do not compare enough
  behavior against vanilla for movement/physics parity.
- `.analysis/server.jar`, `.analysis/protocol-dump.txt`, and `wire-probe` are the
  local-only oracle inputs. Mojang bytes and captures remain gitignored.

## Non-Goals

- M43 does not fix physics unless a tiny harness-only helper is needed.
- M43 does not claim bit-perfect redstone, vehicles, boats, projectiles, or full
  vanilla movement engine parity.
- M43 does not import Mojang data or captures into git.
- M43 does not accept tests that pass by construction on manufactured state while
  bypassing play/runtime code.

## Strategy

1. Capture vanilla first, then encode Solaris assertions.
2. Separate protocol-visible behavior from internal physics constants.
3. Prefer small deterministic worlds and repeatable packet scripts.
4. Mark every comparison as exact, tolerance-based, or intentionally non-goal.
5. Produce a ranked mismatch list for M44, with reproduction commands and expected
   vanilla observations.

## Sub-Milestones

### M43.a - Oracle inventory and capture protocol

Define the local oracle workflow and capture format for physics cases.

Acceptance:

- Document exact commands for starting vanilla 26.1.2 and Solaris test servers.
- `wire-probe` or harness scripts can record relevant clientbound/serverbound
  frames for a named scenario.
- Captures are written under `.analysis/` and not committed.
- Each committed test cites the oracle source path or `javap` class used.

### M43.b - Deterministic physics test worlds

Prepare reproducible world fixtures without committing Mojang bytes.

Acceptance:

- Test setup can create or load deterministic pools, ledges, sugar cane columns,
  drop targets, fluid edges, and mob pens.
- Worlds are generated from repo-owned code or local sidecar data.
- Harness tests skip clearly when required local sidecars are absent.
- No `.analysis/` or `data/vanilla/` payloads enter git.

### M43.c - Player water and swimming oracle

Capture and codify vanilla behavior for entering water, swimming, surfacing, and
leaving water.

Acceptance:

- Captures include walking into shallow water, entering deep water, sprint-swim,
  looking up/down while swimming, jump/shift in water, surfacing, and leaving.
- The harness records the relevant `ServerboundPlayerInput`, player command,
  movement, metadata, velocity/correction, and chunk/fluid state interactions.
- Solaris tests fail or pass against explicit expected signals: metadata, motion,
  corrections, fall damage suppression, and fluid overlap.
- M44 receives a precise mismatch list; no speculative fix is made in M43.

### M43.d - Entity and aquatic physics oracle

Validate vanilla-observable movement for passive mobs, hostile mobs, fish/squid-like
water mobs, and item entities in/near water.

Acceptance:

- Captures include at least one land passive, one hostile, one aquatic fish-like
  entity, and item drops in water and on land.
- Tests distinguish AI goal choice from collision/fluid physics and packet dispatch.
- Assertions cover movement cadence, velocity/motion packets where present,
  despawn/removal, and visible orientation where relevant.
- Existing mob presence tests remain real wire tests.

### M43.e - Block support and break physics oracle

Validate block behavior that currently causes manual pain or likely parity drift.

Acceptance:

- Scenarios cover sugar cane support/breaking, sand/gravel fall start, farmland
  trampling/fall damage edge cases, water/lava replacement, and crop/support rules.
- For each case, record vanilla block updates, drops, item timing, scheduled ticks,
  and persistence expectations.
- Solaris harness tests exercise real block edit/scheduled tick paths.

### M43.f - Item drop and pickup timing oracle

Capture visible drop behavior for survival mining and entity/mob drops.

Acceptance:

- Captures record `AddEntity`, item metadata, pickup delay/window,
  `TakeItemEntity`, `RemoveEntities`, and inventory updates.
- Tests include positive survival drops and negative creative/no-loot cases.
- The visible window is compared with tolerance rather than a hardcoded guess unless
  vanilla proves a fixed value.

### M43.g - Collision and fall-damage oracle

Validate a small set of player/entity collision outcomes against vanilla.

Acceptance:

- Scenarios cover flat ground, one-block step-up if claimed, wall collision,
  water entry fall damage suppression, landing fall damage, and entity AABB basics.
- Tests separate client-predicted movement from server corrections and damage
  packets.
- Any unsupported case is explicitly classified as deferred instead of silently
  passing.

### M43.h - Mismatch report and M44 backlog

Produce the fix backlog that M44 will implement.

Acceptance:

- Each mismatch has a reproduction command, vanilla observation, Solaris
  observation, severity, impacted crates, and proposed fix boundary.
- M44 sub-milestones are updated if the mismatch list changes priority.
- Manual PrismLauncher observations from the owner are included as first-class
  evidence when they differ from harness behavior.

### M43.i - Closeout

Record oracle coverage, skipped/deferred cases, validation, and M44 handoff.

Acceptance:

- `cargo fmt --all -- --check` passes.
- `cargo test --workspace` passes.
- `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings` passes.
- Closeout lists exact oracle captures used, without committing the captures.

## Implementation Notes

- M43.a added `docs/physics-oracles.md` and extended `wire-probe` with
  `--scenario`, `--server-kind`, and `--out` so named captures can be written
  directly under `.analysis/physics-oracles/` with a stable text header.
- M43.b added `tests/physics_validation.rs`, which builds a deterministic
  repo-owned fixture from local `data/vanilla/reports/blocks.json`: shallow and
  deep water, a ledge/collision platform, sugar cane support, sand/gravel/anvil
  fall targets, farmland, adjacent water/lava fluid sources, and a simple mob
  pen. The test skips clearly when the sidecar is absent.
- The fixture smoke test starts a real Solaris server, reaches Play, and drains
  the spawn chunk, keeping M43 validation on runtime/harness code paths.
- M43.c scaffolding added serverbound capture lines and a scripted
  `player-shallow-water-entry` probe path. The Solaris harness now observes the
  real water-input path through a runtime `SetEntityMotion` packet; vanilla
  capture comparison is still pending local oracle execution.
- M43.e scaffolding added a sugar-cane support break observation on the same
  deterministic fixture. It exercises the real creative block-edit path and
  records the target flowing-water replacement plus `BlockChangedAck`. The
  external vanilla harness gate proved vanilla uses `minecraft:water[level=1]`
  for the support cell; Solaris was updated to match that oracle. Sugar cane
  cascade/drop parity remains pending vanilla capture and M44 fix classification.

## Not Done / Deferred To Future Milestones

M43 intentionally stopped at oracle/capture scaffolding plus Solaris runtime
observations. No broad physics fixes landed, and no local vanilla capture files
were committed.

Deferred to M44:

- Core category coverage must be explicit, not implied by one or two examples:
  A. player/entity collision, B. water/fluid spread and replacement,
  C. falling blocks (`sand`, `gravel`, `anvil` and selected related blocks),
  D. player/entity falling and fall damage, E. swimming/water movement.
- Collision coverage must include three separate classes: entity-vs-block,
  entity-vs-fluid, and entity-vs-entity. The first two have `mc-physics` primitive
  tests; entity-vs-entity collision/push is not implemented in Solaris yet and
  needs a vanilla-backed M44 harness before any gameplay claim.
- Player water and swimming parity: run vanilla captures for
  `player-shallow-water-entry`, `player-deep-water-swim`, and
  `player-water-surface-exit`; compare metadata, motion, server corrections,
  fall damage suppression, and fluid overlap against the current Solaris
  `SetEntityMotion` observation. Then fix the proven mismatch in `mc-net` /
  `mc-physics`.
- Sugar cane support and block break parity: capture vanilla support-break timing,
  cascade updates, drops, scheduled ticks, and persistence. M43 now has a real
  vanilla-backed support-cell flowing-water replacement assertion plus
  `BlockChangedAck`; cane cascade and drop behavior are not asserted.
- Item drop and pickup timing: capture and codify `AddEntity`, item metadata,
  pickup delay/window, `TakeItemEntity`, `RemoveEntities`, and inventory updates
  for survival mining, mob drops, and creative/no-loot negatives. This was not
  added in M43 because doing it without full loot/items sidecars would create
  manufactured assertions.
- Entity and aquatic physics: capture at least one land passive, one hostile, one
  aquatic fish-like entity, and item entities in/near water. Existing mob tests
  remain real wire tests, but M43 did not add vanilla comparison captures for AI
  goal choice, water collision, motion cadence, or orientation.
- Collision and fall damage: capture flat ground, wall collision, selected
  one-block step-up, landing fall damage, and water-entry fall damage
  suppression. M43 did not add these assertions beyond the fixture ledge.
- Fluid interaction parity: capture water/lava replacement, source/flowing states,
  underwater breaking, and scheduled fluid ticks. The current support-break smoke
  only observes one water-replacement result.
- Mismatch report: convert the above captures into a ranked list with reproduction
  command, vanilla observation, Solaris observation, severity, impacted crates,
  and proposed fix boundary before any M44 gameplay fix.

Deferred beyond M44 unless selected during M44 triage:

- Bit-perfect redstone, boats, vehicles, projectiles, and full vanilla movement
  engine parity remain out of scope.
- Low-priority mob pathfinding polish, exact despawn edge cases, and broad
  scheduled-tick parity should become a later milestone only after the owner-facing
  M40/M41 manual gaps are fixed.
- Performance follow-up for any physics fix that regresses TPS/lock metrics should
  be split into a separate hardening milestone rather than expanding M44.

## Risks / Pitfalls

- Vanilla movement is partly client-predicted; packet comparisons must distinguish
  prediction from server authority.
- Tolerances can hide real bugs if they are too loose.
- Synthetic worlds can accidentally remove the condition that causes the bug.
- Captures can become stale if the local Mojang jar changes patch version.
- M43 can grow without fixing anything; closeout must hand a bounded backlog to
  M44.
