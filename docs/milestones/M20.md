# M20 - Survival loop foundation

**Status:** in progress on `main` after local tag `m19` under YOLO mode.
Manual gate remains disabled; automated cargo gates are authoritative.
**Owning crates:** `mc-protocol` for survival packets, `mc-net` for player
session survival state, future `mc-entity`/`mc-data` slices for combat, drops,
recipes, and progression.

## Goal

Start the survival loop requested for vanilla-like gameplay: health, food,
saturation, damage/combat hooks, recipes, drops, and progression-critical
mechanics. M20 begins with wire-correct health/food state and keeps later combat
and recipe work on top of tested primitives.

## Sub-Milestones

### M20.a - Health and hunger packet oracle

Add the verified clientbound health/food/saturation packet and seed initial
survival state on Play entry.

Acceptance:

- Packet id and layout are cited from protocol dump + `javap`.
- Byte/round-trip tests pin health, food, and saturation encoding.
- Play entry sends initial full health/food state.

### M20.b - Damage state seam

Add server-side player health mutation APIs with clamping, death threshold, and
future damage-source hooks.

### M20.c - Hunger tick seam

Add exhaustion/saturation/food mutation primitives without yet claiming full
vanilla exhaustion parity.
